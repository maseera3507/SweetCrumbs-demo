Questions people ask

How early should I order?

At least 2 days ahead. Order 3 or more days ahead and the 5% early-bird discount applies automatically.

What do the sugar levels mean?

Regular is our usual sweetness. Less sugar tastes noticeably milder and suits kids and older guests. No refined sugar uses natural sweeteners, so the cake can be a little denser. It costs ₹100 more per kg.

How does payment work?

We confirm your order on WhatsApp. You pay a 50% advance by UPI and the rest on delivery.

Can I change my order after sending it?

Yes, until 24 hours before your date. Just message us on WhatsApp.

I have an allergy. What should I do?

Write it in the note before you send. Tell us about nuts, dairy or gluten and we’ll tell you honestly what we can and can’t do.

How long does the cake stay fresh?

Keep it in the fridge and eat it within 2 days. Take it out 20 minutes before cutting.

Do you deliver?

Yes, across Chennai for a small delivery fee. You can also pick it up from us.
